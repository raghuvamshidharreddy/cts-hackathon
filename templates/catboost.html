Why CatBoost for Car Price Prediction?

🔀
Handles Categorical Data Natively

Car features like Brand, Model, Fuel Type and Transmission are categorical. CatBoost is specifically optimized for categorical variables, encoding them internally without requiring manual one-hot encoding at prediction time.

📈
Captures Non-Linear Relationships

Car pricing is not purely linear. A 2021 BMW X5 with 10,000 miles is worth far more than a 2010 Kia Rio with 200,000 miles — but the relationship is complex. CatBoost's ensemble of trees captures these interactions effectively.

🛡️
Resistant to Overfitting

CatBoost uses ordered boosting and symmetric trees to reduce overfitting compared to standard gradient boosting. This improves its ability to generalise to new car listings not seen during training.

⚡
Fast Inference

Once trained, CatBoost generates predictions in milliseconds — ideal for a real-time web application like Car Sence where users expect instant price estimates after form submission.
