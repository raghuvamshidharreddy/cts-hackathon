03
Lasso Regression Model
A linear regression model with L1 penalty for feature selection

Lasso Regression applies a linear weighted combination of features with automatic feature selection. The L1 penalty shrinks less important feature coefficients to zero, keeping only the most influential features for price prediction.

📊
Linear Formula
Price = w₁×Year + w₂×Mileage + w₃×Brand + ... + w₅₂×Feature₅₂
✂️
L1 Penalty
Shrinks coefficients of low-impact features toward zero, reducing model complexity
🎯
Optimal Fit
Balances prediction accuracy with model simplicity for better generalization

w₁×Year + w₂×Mileage + w₃×Brand + ··· = Final Price

Each feature's weight (coefficient) indicates its impact on the final price. Larger weights mean stronger influence on the prediction.

Why Lasso Regression for Car Price Prediction?

🔀
Strong Linear Relationship

Car pricing exhibits a strong linear relationship with Year and Mileage. Lasso Regression exploits this linear correlation effectively, achieving near-perfect R² scores and excellent RMSE values on car price prediction data.

✂️
Automatic Feature Selection

Lasso's L1 penalty automatically identifies and eliminates irrelevant features by shrinking their coefficients to zero. This keeps the model interpretable and focused on the features that truly matter for price prediction.

🛡️
Resistant to Overfitting

By penalizing model complexity, Lasso avoids overfitting — a common issue in machine learning. It generalizes well to new, unseen car listings not encountered during training.

⚡
Fast Inference & Simple

Lasso generates predictions in microseconds with minimal computation. Its simplicity makes it ideal for real-time applications like Car Sence, where users expect instant price estimates after submitting the form.
